Brand Story
Brand Overview
Founded in 1896, ROYAL HORSE has long been rooted in the spirits trade, building a legacy of curated selection, quality judgment and brand character.

Brand History

ROYAL HORSE has been engaged in the spirits industry since 1896. Through long-term accumulation, the brand reflects an established legacy shaped by experience, continuity and trust.

Product Structure

Our portfolio is built around selected quality spirits across multiple styles and formats, creating a broader and more complete product structure for display, gifting and market presentation.

Brand Style

We are committed to creating products that combine texture and taste, balancing visual expression, packaging character and product presence into a more refined brand experience.

Our Goal

Our goal is to bring a richer and higher-quality selection of spirits to the market, with a more complete portfolio and a stronger sense of long-term brand identity.
